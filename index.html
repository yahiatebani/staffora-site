None of this needs an engineer.

It needs somebody at a desk. Whether you run a team or work on your own, these four slip first while you are on the tools, and every one of them costs you money.

  1. 01
    A customer rings and nobody answers

    You are up a ladder or under a counter. They do not leave a message. They ring the next number on Google, and that job is gone.

  2. 02
    The report goes out days late

    The job is done, but the service report is still in the van. The client chases you for it, and starts to wonder what else gets missed.

  3. 03
    The invoice waits for the weekend

    Invoices get done when you finally sit down. Work done in March, paid in May, and your cash is tied up in jobs you finished weeks ago.

  4. 04
    Nobody knows who owes what

    Part payments land, reminders never go out, and nobody checks the ledger. By the time you look, the debt is three months old and twice as hard to collect.

Every job, handled the same day.

Four things have to happen to every job. All four happen while you are working, not overnight.

  1. 08:10
    The call comes in

    A chiller goes down. Logged, engineer sent, customer given a time. You carry on with the next job.

  2. 11:30
    Access is sorted

    Permit, keys and site contact arranged before the van leaves. Not discovered at the door.

  3. 14:00
    Paperwork chased

    Job sheet, photos and certificate collected while the engineer still remembers the job.

  4. 16:45
    Invoice goes out

    Signed off and invoiced the same day. The chase is already in the diary.

An office eight hours ahead opens when you close. All four of these wait until tomorrow.

One role, trained properly.

Rather than a general assistant who can turn a hand to anything, you get a coordinator who already understands planned maintenance, certificates and chasing subcontractors.

Customer calls are answered and booked in during your working day. Cold calling and your out of hours line stay with you. We would rather tell you the boundary than let you discover it.

Questions we get asked.

The five that decide it. Who manages the work is set out in the table above, and what happens if it does not work out is the guarantee.

Are they employees or freelancers?

Employees. Every team member is employed by Beyondesk in Morocco on a proper contract and works for one client only. We handle payroll, local employment costs, equipment and HR.

What hours do they work?

Your working day. Morocco stays within an hour of UK time all year, so your coordinator is at their desk through your business day. Nobody works a night shift to cover you.

How good is their English?

It varies, which is why we test it rather than claim it. Every candidate arrives with a dated written and spoken assessment, and you interview them before anyone starts. We recruit from the minority of Moroccan graduates with strong English and pay accordingly, rather than hiring at the bottom of the market.

What about our data?

Morocco has no UK adequacy decision, and neither do the Philippines or South Africa, so this applies to any offshore arrangement. Before anyone is given access to your systems, we put a UK International Data Transfer Agreement in place with you, and you decide which systems they can reach.

Is there a long contract?

No. Month to month after onboarding, one month notice, no automatic renewal and no cancellation penalty.

Tell us what the role needs to cover.

A short call about your systems, your paperwork and your hours. We come back with a written cost for the role and a straight answer on whether this fits, which is yours to keep either way.
