Why a trade specific role

A general virtual assistant does not know that an F-Gas leak check interval is set by system charge, that a C2 observation needs quoting rather than filing, or that a gas safety record expiring next week is a problem today. That knowledge is the difference between somebody you have to supervise and somebody who tells you what is coming.

So we train on it before you meet them, and you interview them yourself before you commit to anything.

Not sure which fits

If the role is mostly diary, booking, chasing and invoicing, that is the Administrator tier at £995. If it owns a planned maintenance calendar and compliance expiry across a portfolio of sites, that is the Coordinator tier at £1,395.

If your trade is not listed, ring anyway. The pattern is the same wherever engineers go out in vans and paperwork comes back late.
