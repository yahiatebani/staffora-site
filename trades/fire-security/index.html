The problem this solves.

Fire and security maintenance is a calendar with legal consequences attached. Testing intervals are set by standard rather than convenience, the asset register has to stay accurate as devices are added and replaced, and every remedial left open is evidence sitting in the wrong column when a responsible person asks for it.

Testing intervals, access arrangements and responsible person requests all run on UK time. A missed interval is not a scheduling inconvenience, so the person tracking it should be awake when you are.

What they handle.

Statutory testing calendarsMaintains the testing schedule to the intervals each standard requires and books ahead rather than in arrears.
Asset register maintenanceKeeps device registers current as panels, detectors and units are added, moved or replaced.
Remedial trackingLogs defects from every visit, raises quotes and chases decisions so nothing sits open without an owner.
Compliance evidenceAssembles the certificates and test records responsible persons and insurers ask for.

The paperwork they track

  • Fire alarm servicing and test records to BS 5839 intervals
  • Emergency lighting test records, including annual duration tests to BS 5266
  • Extinguisher servicing records to BS 5306
  • Intruder and access control maintenance visit records
  • Device and asset registers by site
  • Defect and remedial logs with quote and decision status
